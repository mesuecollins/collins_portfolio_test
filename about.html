About Me

I am a Computer Engineer with a unique "full-stack" perspective that spans from the silicon level to the cloud. My journey began with a strong foundation in Electrical & Electronic Engineering at the University of Buea and culminated in a BSc in Computer Engineering from Kampala International University.

As the founder of MacZ Repair, I diagnose and fix complex hardware issues at the component level. Simultaneously, I architect scalable cloud solutions as a Google Certified Associate Cloud Engineer and build cross-platform mobile experiences using the latest in Kotlin Multiplatform technology.

My goal represents the convergence of hardware excellence and modern software engineering—building systems that are robust from the circuit board to the Kubernetes cluster.
